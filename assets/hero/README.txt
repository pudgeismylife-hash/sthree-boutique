THE HERO FILM
=============

Drop a file called  hero.mp4  in this folder and the front page plays it
full-screen behind the words. Nothing else needs changing.

Until that file exists the page shows hero-still.jpg instead, so the shop
is never broken and never opens on a black rectangle.

WHAT TO SHOOT
  A model walking, or turning, wearing pieces from the collection.
  10 to 15 seconds is plenty -- it loops, so it should end roughly where
  it began or the jump will show.
  Shot wide (landscape), not portrait: the hero is a wide band and a
  portrait clip is cropped hard at the top and bottom.
  No sound needed. It plays muted, because every phone refuses to
  autoplay anything else.

THE FILE
  hero.mp4, H.264, 1920x1080 or 1280x720.
  Keep it under about 8 MB. It sits in the repository and every visitor
  downloads it, so a 60 MB file makes the front page slow for everyone.
  Handbrake or any phone editor will export this.

hero-still.jpg is what shows while the film loads, if the film is
missing, and to anyone who has asked their machine to stop things moving.
Replace it with a still from the film once there is one -- it is the
palazzo cordset photograph at the moment, with its cream margins trimmed
off so it reaches the edges.
