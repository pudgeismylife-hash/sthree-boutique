## Bags

One record, **not published**: `mk-signature-satchel`, Michael Kors signature
satchel, ₹3,500, 10 × 8 inches, `authenticity:"not verified"`, `hold:true`,
no images stored.

In `product-image-lock.json` as **`SB-BAG-01` / `PENDING_VERIFICATION`**, holding
the claim, the fact that no evidence was supplied, the four indicators that
prompted the doubt, and what unblocks it.

Nothing is disguised — the name keeps the brand. Nothing is asserted —
authenticity is recorded as unverified, not denied and not claimed.

**Not done:** public card, product page, link preview, schema entry, stored
images. Those are the trademark-use surface.

**To publish:** produce a source (invoice, distributor, equivalent). Then delete
`hold:true`, add the photographs, rebuild — it lists in full with the brand name
and logo intact. Reselling genuine stock is lawful; the block is the absence of
provenance, not the price.

## How to work on it

`sthree-boutique.html` is the only file to edit by hand. Everything else is
generated:

```
node build-hosted.js          index.html, p/*.html, sw.js, share + hosted copies
python3 build-og-images.py --apply   link-preview cards, hero crops, thumbnails
python3 build-tiles.py --apply       the five category tiles
python3 import-updated-photos.py --apply   bring in a new photo batch
python3 clean-backgrounds.py --apply       cut a flat white field off a picture
```

Guards worth knowing, all added after something went wrong:

- the hero refuses any piece still `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, and prints how many of its
  twelve are recreations
- a category tile must come from a source with a transparent original, and
  refuses a `REVIEW_REQUIRED` piece unless the tile carries an explicit exception
- held rows are stripped from all three built files, so a held piece does not
  ship even as data
- `build-hosted.js` sweeps stub pages and preview cards for pieces no longer shown

## Traps this project has already fallen into

- **A review record is not a review state.** Two builds tested for the presence of
  `sourceReview` rather than `state === "REVIEW_REQUIRED"`, and silently refused
  settled pieces. The tile build failed all morning on 18 Aug because of it, and
  the front page kept the previous day's pictures while looking like a cache
  problem.
- **Tiles are composites and never refresh themselves.** Replacing a product photo
  leaves its category tile on the old picture.
- **Cream is not one colour.** Images flattened on `#FAF6F0` sit on a `#F3EDE4`
  stage and show a seam. Transparent WebP is why every product on sale has a
  `.webp` beside its `.jpg`.
- **`alpha:true` demands a `.webp` for every view**, not just the first, or the
  gallery requests a file that was never written.
- **`Image.getbbox()` trims black, not cream.** Use the difference-mask `trim_box`.
- **github.io is unreachable from the build container.** Deployment can be
  confirmed from the Actions run and by rendering the built file; the live URL
  itself cannot be fetched here. Cache complaints need `?v=N` to settle.
- **The container can come back with a stale clone.** On 19 Aug it returned at the
  previous day's commit; `origin` was correct. Check `git rev-parse HEAD` against
  `origin/main` before trusting any local reading.
