About the boutique

Sthree Boutique is Priyanka Monteiro's shop in Bikarnakatte, Mangalore.

Western wear, ethnic wear, co-ord sets, anti-tarnish jewellery, press-on nails and bags — imported and Indian, chosen for quality, for how they look, and for the personality they carry.

The shop is open every day, nine in the morning until eight at night, and you are welcome to come in and try things on. If you cannot, message on WhatsApp and Priyanka will send you more photographs of anything here before you decide.

Delivered all over India, and worldwide.
